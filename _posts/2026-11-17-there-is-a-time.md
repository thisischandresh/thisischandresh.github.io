There is a time, a place, a person, a feeling  
The time will pass  
The place will be lost  
The person will go  
The feeling will change

There is a time, a place, a friend, a memory  
The time will pause  
The place will freeze  
The friend will stay  
The memory will change

There is a time, an age, a relationship, a home  
The time will fade  
The age will decay  
The relationship will end  
The home will change

There is a time, a lake, a brother, a fight  
The time never was  
The lake doesn't exist  
The brother is no more  
The fight has changed  

There is a time, a place, a person, a feeling  
The time is now  
The place is here  
The person is me  
The feeling is in me  
The life will be
